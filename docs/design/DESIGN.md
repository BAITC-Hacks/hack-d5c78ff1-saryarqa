# City simulator: mint and green
## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
User-provided furniture UI reference: airy mint surfaces, vivid green curved panels, compact black typography. Adapt to a desktop city simulator.
## 2. Color
Canvas #e8f4ed; surfaces #ffffff and #f6faf7; accent #00c853; ink #17241d; secondary #57695e; borders #dce9df. Keep geographic layer colors unchanged.
## 3. Typography
Segoe UI / system sans serif; 48–60px desktop hero, 32px mobile, 14px body, 12px controls. Dark text on bright green ensures contrast.
## 4. Spacing & Grid
Centered maximum 1500px shell; 24px desktop gutters, 16px mobile. Sections 32–48px apart; cards 20–24px padding.
## 5. Layout & Composition
Header city selector at far right. Green organic hero, compact metrics, map as the largest work area. Planner follows map; no furniture images.
## 6. Components
Cards radius 16–24px; black and green buttons radius 10px; native accessible city select. Keep minimal place cards and remove How to play header link.
## 7. Motion & Interaction
Short color transitions; reduced-motion support. No decorative looping motion. City changes load actual city maps and keep Astana-only model clearly separate.
## 8. Voice & Brand
Concise Russian interface. Astana, Almaty, Shymkent. Real sources; synthetic scoring only where the original dataset exists.
## 9. Anti-patterns
No invented buildings or city statistics. No reference logo, furniture content, voucher claims, unnecessary captions, or changes to map colors.
