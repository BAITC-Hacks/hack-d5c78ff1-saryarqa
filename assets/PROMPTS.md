# Raster generation briefs

The agents used built-in imagegen for the raster concept previews and for character/vehicle source sprites. The texts below are the normalized final creative briefs used for this package; approved revisions are indicated. The geography reference sheet was drawn from cited facts in SVG and was not AI-generated.

| Agent | Brief and approved revision |
|---|---|
| 01 style | Warm miniature city, one illustrative block in coordinated top-down and tilted 2.5D views; river, bridge, road, park, buildings and bus lane. Revision v2: saturated flat color blocks, fewer trees/windows, minimal texture, no labels or real district outlines. |
| 03 world | Matching top-down and tilted terrain, grass, plaza, sidewalk, road networks, bus lane, crossing, river edge, bridge, trees, shrubs, park, bench and streetlight; warm miniature style, consistent footprint and anchors. |
| 04 buildings | Nine types of miniature buildings and urban cues in matching top-down and tilted views. Revision v2: bold, saturated, simple silhouettes, very few details, readable at small map size. |
| 05 characters | Three alternative character treatments for the same fictional mayor, citizens, maintenance worker and emergency responder. Approved option A: rounded miniature figures with simple readable clothing and gestures, no likeness of a real official. Final source sprites were generated from this direction. |
| 06 vehicles | Bus, car, LRT and service van in top-down and tilted views. Revision v2: saturated colors, large simple shapes, few details, consistent forward direction; transparent final sprites. |
| 07 policies M01–M07 | For each measure: one UI icon, one map pin, and one simple 2.5D cue. Revision v2: reduced texture and detail, larger recognizable symbols, no numerical outcomes. |
| 08 policies M08–M14 | For each measure: one UI icon, one map pin, and one simple 2.5D cue, saturated colors and readable silhouettes, no numerical outcomes. |
| 09 HUD | Five category icons, ten indicator icons, gameplay/map controls, and three app icon concepts. Approved app icon A (city); final SVGs simplified for 24–32 px and contain no turn number. |
| 10 effects | Simple saturated map selection, placement, route, improvement, warning, reaction and finish effects, with static alternatives for reduced motion and no baked score. |

Raster previews are in `previews/`. The game-ready vectors are redrawn as editable SVG from the approved briefs; raster previews are not used as geographic evidence.
