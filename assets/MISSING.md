# Missing geographic assets

## Current six-district polygons and city outline

**Status: missing / unverified.** The publicly visible city GIS layer contains six polygons, but its areas differ from the official 2026 district areas; it has no visible revision date or reuse license. Six Astana OpenStreetMap relations were also checked through Nominatim under ODbL, and their areas differ substantially from the official 2026 values. Neither set can be used as final district geometry. Do not package either set of polygons, a city outline or six masks derived from them, or a tilted version as final geography. Source audit: [RESEARCH-REPORT.md](RESEARCH-REPORT.md).
